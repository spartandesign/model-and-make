Filament: PLA, PETG & TPU

Filament is the plastic string the printer melts. We use three kinds. PLA is easy and stiff, PETG is tougher, and TPU is rubbery. Pick the one your part needs, and keep it dry.

You don’t type these numbers in. Bambu Studio’s filament profiles (like Bambu PLA Basic or Generic PETG) set them for you. Knowing them helps you spot a wrong setting.

Read the spool

Every spool label tells you the type (PLA, PETG, TPU), the diameter (ours are all 1.75 mm), the weight (usually 1 kg), and a temperature range. Bambu spools have a chip the AMS reads, so it knows the type and color automatically. Other brands need you to set the type on the screen.

Load and unload

Goal: filament ready to feed

Cut a clean end

Snip the end at an angle with flush cutters so it slides in easily. Never let go of the loose end: it springs back and tangles.

✅ Check it: a sharp, straight, clean end.

Goal: PLA or PETG in the AMS

Load the AMS

Put the spool in an AMS slot and push the end into the feeder until it grabs. The AMS pulls it in. On the screen, check the slot shows the right type and color.

✅ Check it: the slot’s color and type match the spool.

Goal: TPU on the spool holder

Load an external spool

Hang the spool on the holder on the back. Feed the end into the tube by hand, then use Load on the screen. TPU never goes in the AMS.

✅ Check it: filament comes out of the nozzle when it loads.

Goal: a tidy spool

Unload and store

Use Unload on the screen. Push the end through a hole on the edge of the spool so it can’t unwind. Bag it with desiccant.

✅ Check it: the end is clipped, and the spool is back in its bin.

Keep it dry

All filament soaks up water from the air, a little every day. Wet filament pops, strings, and prints weak, brittle parts. TPU and PETG are the most picky.

  • Store spools in sealed bins or bags with desiccant packs.
  • If filament snaps when you bend it, or the print is stringy and bubbly, it’s probably wet. Tell your teacher. It can be dried in a filament dryer.

⚠️ Safety

  • Use flush cutters, cutting away from you.
  • The nozzle is still hot while filament loads. Keep fingers away.
  • Desiccant packs are not food. Keep them in the bins.
