Flux Ador Laser

The Ador uses a blue diode laser, like a super-powerful laser pointer, to cut and engrave fine detail. It has a camera to show where your design will land and an autofocus probe to set the height for you.

How it’s set up in our lab

  • The vent hose is attached and goes outside or to a filter. The Ador must never run without it.
  • Beam Studio is installed on the lab computers and connects to the Ador over Wi-Fi (or USB).
  • Aluminum foil lines the bottom. The triangular prisms (or a honeycomb) lift your material so the beam doesn’t scorch the bottom.
  • The camera has been calibrated, so the preview lines up with the real bed.

Beam Studio: from file to cut

Goal: a material ready to cut

Load and focus

Set your material on the prisms. Close the door. Long-press AF on the panel (or double-click the left focus button) to autofocus.

✅ Check it: the probe touched the material and went back up.

Goal: the right machine

Connect

In Beam Studio, choose the Ador, and select the 20 W laser module.

✅ Check it: Beam Studio shows the Ador as connected.

Goal: a picture of your material

Camera preview

Click the camera preview icon (top left) and drag over the area where your material is. Beam Studio shows a photo of the bed.

✅ Check it: you can see your material on screen.

Goal: your design in place

Import and place

Import your SVG or DXF (from TinkerCAD or Onshape). Drag it onto the material in the photo.

✅ Check it: the whole design is on the material, away from the edges.

Goal: the right settings

Choose parameters

Set each layer to cut or engrave and pick a parameter preset for your material and thickness. Ask your teacher if your material isn’t listed.

✅ Check it: cut lines and engrave areas have different settings.

Goal: a running job

Send and watch

Click the send arrow, check the preview time, and start. Stay with it until it finishes, then wait for the smoke to clear before opening the door.

✅ Check it: you watched the whole job.

Safety

It looks or sounds scary…What’s really going on
A very bright blue spotThat’s the cut. The tinted door blocks the beam, so watching through it is safe. Never look at the beam with the door open (it can’t fire then anyway).
Fan noise and smokeThe exhaust and air assist doing their job.
The probe pokes your materialAutofocus measuring the height. It touches lightly.

⚠️ What can actually hurt you

  • Fire. FLUX’s own rule: never leave a job unattended, and keep an extinguisher nearby.
  • Fumes from banned materials. Approved list only, vent hose attached.
  • Never block or trick the door sensor.
  • Emergency? Press the red E-stop on the right side.
