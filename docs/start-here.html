Getting Started

Start Here: How This Course Works

The four rails

You will use a paper notebook for thinking, Onshape for CAD, OneNote for your portfolio, and Canvas to turn work in. Always start in Canvas.

Set up your engineering notebook (Week 1)

Number the pages, save the first two for a table of contents, put your name and period on the cover, and date every entry. You never hand it in — it is for thinking, not neatness.

Your OneNote page (every deliverable)

Each project gets one OneNote page with four parts: the design, the made part (photo with a caliper in frame), designed vs. measured, and a short reflection.

Keep two records that feed a third. The paper notebook is for thinking; OneNote is the curated portfolio (one page per deliverable: the design, the made part, designed vs. measured, a reflection); Canvas is where you turn the graded version in.
