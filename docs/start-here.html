Getting Started

Start Here: How This Course Works

The four rails

You will use Onshape for CAD, a Google Slides engineering notebook for your design work, Google Docs for written work, and Canvas to turn work in. Always start in Canvas.

Set up your engineering notebook (Week 1)

Make your copy of the notebook template (sign in with your school Google account and click Make a copy). Name it P1 Last First — Engineering Notebook and share it with your teacher. Keep the same copy all semester. Paper is still great for quick sketches and raw numbers: photograph them onto your slides.

Your notebook slides (every deliverable)

Each project gets four slides, color-coded by phase: Define (the goal and limits), Develop (sketches, CAD, decisions), Optimize (the made part, with a caliper in the photo, and designed vs. measured), and Reflect (what worked and what you’d change). Sign and date every slide. Short written work goes in a Google Doc instead. See Your Engineering Notebook.
