Dremel LC40 Laser Cutter

The LC40 uses an invisible infrared beam, focused to a tiny spot, to burn a path through wood, acrylic, and card. Mirrors guide the beam, the lens focuses it, air blows the smoke away, and a fan sends it outside.

How it works

  1. Electricity excites the CO₂ gas in the tube, which gives off a strong infrared beam.
  2. Three mirrors bounce the beam across the machine to the laser head.
  3. The lens focuses it to a tiny, very hot spot. The material at that spot burns or melts away.
  4. The head moves on the gantry to trace your design. Cut lines go all the way through. Engrave areas only burn the surface.
  5. The beam burns a thin slot called kerf. That’s why your rack slots in Chapter 11 needed a kerf test.

Focus matters most. The spot is sharp only at one height above the material. Set it with the focus puck (spacer) under the head. Out of focus means wide, weak, charred cuts.

How it’s set up in our lab

  • The Hex Box holds the cooling water (distilled only) and supplies air assist. The laser won’t run if the water isn’t flowing.
  • The exhaust hose vents outside. The vent fan runs during every job and for 10 minutes after.
  • The LC40 is on the school network. Open the control software in a browser by typing the IP address shown on the LC40’s screen.
  • A CO₂ fire extinguisher hangs nearby. Nothing flammable is stored on or next to the laser.

Before every job

Goal: a safe machine

Check the machine

Exhaust on, Hex Box running, no red status icons on the screen, honeycomb bed clean, extinguisher in place.

✅ Check it: the water, fan, air, and lid icons are all good.

Goal: an approved material

Check the material

Only from the approved list. It lies flat on the honeycomb. Warped wood can be weighted down at the edges, away from the cut.

✅ Check it: you can name the material, and it’s on the approved list.

Goal: a focused beam

Focus

Move the head over the material. Put the focus puck under the head, lower it until it just touches, then lock it and remove the puck.

✅ Check it: the puck slides out snugly, without pushing the material.

The LC40 software: from file to cut

Goal: the software open

Connect

Type the IP address from the LC40’s screen into the browser’s address bar.

✅ Check it: you see the LC40 control page with the camera view.

Goal: your design on the bed

Import and place

Upload your file. Use SVG or PDF for cut lines (from TinkerCAD or an Onshape drawing exported 1:1 as PDF). Drag it onto your material in the camera view.

✅ Check it: the design sits on your material, inside the edges.

Goal: the right power

Choose settings

Pick your material from the materials library, or ask your teacher for heat, speed, and passes. Set cut lines to cut and filled areas to engrave.

✅ Check it: the material and thickness match what’s on the bed.

Goal: a path that fits

Run perimeter

Click Run Perimeter. The red dot traces the outline of your job.

✅ Check it: the red dot stays on the material the whole way around.

Goal: a running job

Start

Click Start, close the lid, then press the physical Start button twice quickly. Stay and watch through the window.

✅ Check it: the job runs, and small flickers go out right away.

Goal: clean parts, clean air

Finish

When it’s done, wait 30 seconds before lifting the lid so the smoke clears. Leave the fan running 10 minutes. Remove parts and scraps.

✅ Check it: the bed is clear for the next person.

Safety

It looks or sounds scary…What’s really going on
Small flickers of flame at the cutNormal for wood and card, as long as they go out as the head moves on. A flame that grows or stays is not normal.
A bright spot and smokeThat’s the cut. The window blocks the infrared beam, so watching through it is safe.
Humming and bubbling soundsThe water pump and air pump in the Hex Box.
“Can the laser shoot me?”Not with the lid closed. The lid sensor stops the laser if it opens. That’s why you never defeat the lid sensor.

⚠️ What can actually hurt you

  • Fire. Never leave a job running, not even for a minute.
  • Fumes from banned materials. Approved list only, exhaust always on.
  • Hot parts right after a cut, and sharp acrylic edges.
  • The tube and power supply use high voltage. Only your teacher opens panels.
