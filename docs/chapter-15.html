🪚 Start with the Lab Guide

Everything about the machine is on the X-Carve Pro page: the parts diagram, how it’s set up, Easel step by step, common problems, and the license sign-off. This chapter is about using it for your team’s park element.

What your team carves

One wooden obstacle for the class park, from the plywood your teacher gives you. Pick one:

  • A ledge: two or three identical rectangles, cut out and glued into a solid block. Engrave your team number on top.
  • A kicker: three identical wedge-shaped profiles, glued side by side and sanded smooth on the slope.

It sits in the park on its own, so it doesn’t need the class connector. It does need to be real fingerboard scale: measure your deck and trucks like you did for the display stand.

Design rules

  • 180 mm or less on every side, like every park section.
  • One bit: the flat end mill your teacher sets up. No bit changes.
  • 8 minutes or less of carve time in Easel’s preview. That’s how all 12 teams get a turn.
  • Tabs on every through-cut. The team number is a shallow pocket, about 1 mm deep.
  • Measured thickness: caliper the plywood in three spots and enter the average. Labels lie: “1/2 in” plywood is often thinner.

Try it: zero, depth, and tabs

Set the cut depth to the material thickness, then try zeroing on the wasteboard instead of the material. See why the Z-probe goes on top.

Goal: a design Easel understands

Set up Easel

Choose X-Carve Pro. Enter your measured material size and thickness. Choose the bit your teacher set up.

✅ Check it: the thickness in Easel matches your caliper average.

Goal: your element in Easel

Draw it

Draw your rectangles or wedges, side by side with space between them. Set each outline to cut outside the line, all the way through, with tabs. Add your team number as a 1 mm pocket.

✅ Check it: every outline has tabs, and the parts don’t overlap.

Goal: a plan that fits the rules

Preview

Check the 3D preview and the time. Too long? Make it smaller or carve fewer parts. Then show your teacher.

✅ Check it: the time is 8 minutes or less, and your teacher OK’d it.

Goal: a safe carve

Carve with your teacher

Glasses and hearing protection on. Clamp it, home, Z-probe, set X/Y zero, vacuum on, carve. Stay with it. Hands out until the spindle stops.

✅ Check it: your part is cut out, held by its tabs.

Goal: a finished element

Finish it

Snip the tabs, sand the edges, and glue the pieces. Clamp the glue-up until it dries.

✅ Check it: your fingerboard rides it smoothly.

⏱️ The carve rotation

There’s one X-Carve and 12 teams, so carving starts at the end of Tuesday and runs all Thursday. While you wait: sand, plan the glue-up, practice the license steps on the diagram, and start reading the capstone brief in Chapter 16.
