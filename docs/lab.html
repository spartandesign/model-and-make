The Lab & Safety

Your teacher operates every machine; you design and measure but never run a printer or a laser.

The machines

  • Bambu Lab P2S (six at school) — the 3D printers used for every print.
  • Bambu Lab A1 Mini — extra printing capacity. Its 180 mm bed sets the size limit for capstone sections, so any section can print on any printer.
  • Dremel LC40 (CO₂ laser) — the heavy cutter; the only one that cuts clear and colored acrylic.
  • Flux Ador (20 W diode laser) — finer detail; cuts wood, plywood, leather, card, and dark/black acrylic only.
  • X-Carve Pro — in the lab but not used in this course.

Which laser?

  • Clear or colored acrylic → LC40 only.
  • Wood, plywood, leather, card, or dark material → either.
  • Thicker or faster → LC40.

Safety

  • Never reach into a printer or laser while it runs.
  • Lasers: lid closed, fume extraction on, approved materials only, never leave a job unattended.
