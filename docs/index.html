Model & Make

Measure parts to a thousandth of an inch. Model them in Onshape. 3D-print a working fingerboard. Then build one skate park with your whole class. The big idea is tolerance: too tight won't fit, too loose wobbles.

How the lab works

You design and measure. Your teacher runs every machine. Here is the loop:

  1. In class: sketch, measure, and model your part. Submit the file on Canvas.
  2. Between classes: your teacher prints or cuts it. Class doesn't meet Wednesday or Friday, so those are print days.
  3. Next class: your part is back. Caliper it, test the fit, log the result, and plan one change.

See The Lab & Safety for the machines and the safety rules.
