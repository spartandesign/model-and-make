Glossary

The words that run through the course. Each term shows the week it first appears.

Caliper Week 1
A precision tool that reads thickness, diameter, depth, and openings to a thousandth of an inch.
Clearance Week 8
Extra space added on purpose so parts fit: a hole = peg + clearance.
Connection standard Week 14
The shared peg size, spacing, and clearance every capstone team builds to.
Degrees of freedom Week 6
The ways a part can still move after it's mated.
Deviation Week 5
Measured size minus designed size — how far off the machine was.
Extrude Week 4
Pushing a 2D sketch into 3D to give it thickness.
FDM Week 5
Fused Deposition Modeling — how the Bambu printer builds parts, layer by layer.
Interference Week 5
When two parts overlap and won't fit — too tight.
Isometric view Week 2
A single 3D-looking view that shows three faces at once.
Kerf Week 12
The width of material the laser beam removes — so a slot cut exactly to size comes out too big.
Mate Week 6
A relationship that joins parts in an assembly and controls how they move.
Measurement bridge Week 4
An entry linking an on-screen CAD dimension to a real caliper reading.
Orthographic views Week 2
The straight-on front, top, and side views, in correct projection.
Parametric CAD Week 4
Design where dimensions drive the model — change a number and the part updates. You use Onshape.
PLA Week 5
The plastic filament used for printing.
Press-fit (tab-and-slot) Week 13
A joint that holds with no glue because the slot is cut narrower than the material by the kerf.
Revolve Week 6
Spinning a sketch around an axis to make round shapes.
Score Week 3
A shallow laser line that lets material fold without being cut through.
Single-operator loop Week 5
Design in class, the teacher runs the machine between sessions, you measure and iterate next time.
Sketch Week 4
A 2D outline you turn into 3D with features like extrude and revolve.
Slicer Week 5
Software (Bambu Studio) that turns a 3D model into machine instructions.
Slop Week 5
When a fit is too loose and wobbles — the opposite of interference.
Title block Week 2
The labeled corner of a drawing: name, object, date, units.
Tolerance Week 5
The allowed difference between the size you design and the size a machine actually makes.
Vector vs. raster Week 12
A laser cuts/scores along vector lines and engraves images as a raster pattern.
Working drawing Week 11
A complete, dimensioned drawing a manufacturer could build from.
