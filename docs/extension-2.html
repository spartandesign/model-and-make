Gear Ratios for Robots

Optional, for early finishers. Your fingerboard has wheels, and so do robots. This extension shows how robot builders choose gears to trade speed for strength, and has you model two gears that turn together in Onshape.

🧩 PLTW ActivityExtension 2 · when you finish (ask your teacher)

🎯 Your goal

  • I can find a gear ratio as driven : driving.
  • I can predict output speed and torque from the ratio.
  • I can connect two gears in Onshape with a gear relation.

🧰 You’ll need

  • Onshape
  • The gear calculator on this page
  • Notebook for your math

⏳ Time and limits

  • Self-paced: about 2 sessions of 45 minutes.
  • Finish your regular chapter work first. This is extra.
  • VEX gears come in 12, 36, 60, and 84 teeth.

Step-by-step plan

Follow the steps in order. The minutes are a guide. Check off each checkpoint before you move on.

Session 1 · about 45 min

  1. 8 minWatch the video.
  2. 15 minWork three ratios by hand: 60:12, 12:60, and 84:12.
  3. 17 minUse the gear calculator. Find a setup for a fast robot and one for a strong arm.
  4. 5 minWrite both in your notebook.

✔ Checkpoint: Two setups chosen, with the math.

Session 2 · about 45 min

  1. 15 minIn Onshape, make two simple gears (or insert them if your teacher shares the VEX library).
  2. 15 minAdd a revolute mate to each, then a gear relation with your ratio.
  3. 10 minTurn one gear. Check that the other turns the right way and speed.
  4. 5 minTake the self-check.

✔ Checkpoint: Two gears that turn together at your ratio.

The one number

A gear ratio is driven : driving, output teeth over input teeth. A 12-tooth gear driving a 60-tooth gear is 60 : 12 = 5 : 1. The output turns 5 times slower, with 5 times the torque.

  • Output speed = motor speed ÷ ratio
  • Output torque = motor torque × ratio

VEX V5 motors come with cartridges for 100, 200, or 600 RPM. That’s the speed you start from.

Goal: two gears

Make or insert the gears

Model two simple gears (a disk is enough to start), or insert real VEX gears if your teacher shares the library.

✅ Check it: you have two gear parts in an Assembly.

Goal: gears that spin

Add revolute mates

Give each gear a revolute mate on its axle.

✅ Check it: each gear spins on its own.

Goal: gears that turn together

Add a gear relation

Choose Gear relation, pick both revolute mates, and enter your ratio.

✅ Check it: turn one gear. The other turns the opposite way at the right speed.
