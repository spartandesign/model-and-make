Monday — team up and start 4.1

📚 Onshape lessons

Onshape Intro to CAD — Unit 4: Teamwork Makes the Dream Work. Use lessons 4.1–4.4. Each lesson has a slide deck and a student guide.

  • Fill out the team agreement. Who owns which parts? How will you decide? What happens if someone is absent?
  • Split the launcher. Teammate A builds the ring and spinner (4.1). Teammate B builds the key and prop (4.2). Teammate C sets up the assembly and leads branch and merge (4.3–4.4).

Block 1 — build and assemble

  • 4.1 Ring and Spinner. Model in context, so your part fits a teammate’s part. Use sweep, helix, and circular pattern.
  • 4.2 Key and Prop. Use mate connectors, relations, and a linear pattern.
  • 4.3 Assembling the Launcher. Put all the parts together in one assembly.

Block 2 — branch, merge, and set up your park

  • 4.4 Branch and Merge. Try a change on a branch. Then merge it back into the main design.
  • Start your park document. Make one team Onshape document named Team##_SkatePark. Share it with your teammates and your teacher.
  • Bring in the standard. Insert the class connector from Week 14. Every section will use it.
  • Make your branches. Each teammate makes a branch for their sections.

Lesson 4.5 (printing the launcher) is skipped. We are saving printer time for the park.
